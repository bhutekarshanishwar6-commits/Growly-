How to use Growly

A simple guide to help you turn small daily actions into visible progress.

1

Create your goal

Open New Goal, give your garden a name, choose a category, set the duration and write your daily target.

2

Complete one small action

Open your active garden and tap Add Today's Flower whenever you complete your daily action. Your streak, flowers and progress update automatically.

3

Check your improvement

Use Progress to see consistency, completed days, streaks and garden growth. Open any garden for its calendar and improvement points.

4

Keep your profile updated

In Profile & Settings, add your age, gender, address, occupation and other details. These stay on your device in localStorage.

5

Backup your data

Use ↓ Export Progress to save a JSON backup. Use ↑ Import Progress to restore a Growly backup later.

6

Generate your improvement PDF

Go to Progress and choose Generate Improvement PDF. Your profile and recorded progress are prepared as a printable PDF report.

🌱
Remember: Growly is designed around consistency, not perfection. A small completed action is still progress.
